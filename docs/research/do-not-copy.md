# Do Not Copy — Learn the Principle, Not the Design

Phase: 02 — World-Class Benchmark & Translation Research
Date: 2026-10-07
Reference IDs: [benchmark-study.md](benchmark-study.md).

We want original art direction, not a collage. For every reference that informs a mechanism: what we may learn, what we must not copy. References marked X or L1 in the register informed no mechanism and are listed in §3 only.

---

## 1. General rules

1. **Copy mechanisms, never surfaces.** No layouts, colour schemes, typefaces, illustration styles, 3D models, motion signatures, copywriting or brand devices from any reference.
2. **No recognisable homage.** If a reviewer can name the source by looking at a screen, it is too close.
3. **No borrowed assets.** No stock 3D models, textures, fonts, sounds or icons from referenced sites; all assets are licensed or original.
4. **No borrowed wording.** German copy is written for this practice; English references are never translated verbatim.
5. **Art references stay art references.** Calder's mobiles inform the *principle of balance*, not the form language.
