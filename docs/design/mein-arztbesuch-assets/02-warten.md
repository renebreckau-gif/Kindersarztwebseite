## 1. PURPOSE

Normalises waiting. Job: *waiting is part of it and is calm.* There is no clock, no time pressure, and no claim about this practice's waiting room.

## 2. STORY MOMENT

Child sits on a long low bench, looking into a picture book held on the lap (a book from home, as the parent tip suggests). The companion sits beside the child. Copy: „Manchmal wartet man ein bisschen, bis man dran ist. Warten gehört dazu.“

## 3. EMOTION

Settled, absorbed, unhurried. The child's legs dangle, not swinging.

## 4. PRIMARY SUBJECT

**Bench**: long, low warm-ceramic bench with one coral cushion, C2–C10 × R6–R7. Seat top y 1100, front edge y 1200, legs to y 1332. It is drawn slightly in perspective (the left end nearer).

## 5. SECONDARY OBJECTS

- **Child**: seated, centred x 1100 (C5–C6), head top y 520 (head ≥ 180 px), ¾ front view looking down at the book; yellow top. The book has a closed blue-grey cover and shows **no text, no characters, no brand**.
- **Companion**: seated at the left end of the bench, cut by the left frame edge at x ≤ 420 (C1–C2). Visible: knee, forearm resting near the child, cobalt sleeve, coral bag on the bench.
- **Wall light**: one soft round warm light disc on the dark background at C9–C11 × R2–R4 (diameter ~460 px). It is a lamp, **not a clock** (no hands, no marks).

## 6. ENVIRONMENT

Night stage, floor and bench. No other seats, no other people, no toys, no magazines, no aquarium, no plants, no screens.

## 7. COMPOSITION

Horizontal calm: the bench line anchors the lower third; the child sits on the left/centre third line; the wall light balances it in the upper right. The empty bench to the right of the child (C7–C10) says „there is room“.

## 8. CAMERA ANGLE

Child eye height of a seated child (y ≈ 760), 50 mm, straight on to the bench front, tilt 0–3°.

## 9. LIGHT

Key from the upper right falls on the book and the child's face; the wall light adds a soft warm halo but does **not** light the scene. Cobalt fill under the bench.

## 10. COLOR

Night; bench ceramic #EEE9DF; cushion coral #F28B74; child top #F1CF68; companion sleeve #4A5CFF; book cover #8C95B8; wall light #FFE4C4 at low intensity.

## 11. MATERIAL

Ceramic bench (matte-satin), velvet coral cushion, felt clothes, a matte paper book.

## 12. NEGATIVE SPACE

C7–C12 × R1–R5 except the wall light disc; the empty bench C7–C10 × R6 stays empty.

## 13. DESKTOP ASPECT / CROP

Full master. The empty bench and the wall light give the text column breathing room.

## 14. MOBILE ASPECT / CROP

Child head at x 1000–1200, y 520–700 and book at y 840–960: inside the safe zone. The wall light may touch the safe-zone edge (x ≤ 2160).

## 15. SEPARATE LAYERS

`bg` · `floor` · `object` (bench + cushion) · `fx` (wall light, separate) · `figures` (child + companion arm/bag) · `prop` (book, separate, for future variants).

## 16. POTENTIAL MOTION

Optional, once: the wall light slowly brightens (opacity 70 → 100 %, 2 s). There is no page turning, no swinging legs and no loop.

## 17. CLASSIFICATION

**ORIGINAL ART preferred** (commissioned illustrator/3D artist working to the style frame). AI_CONCEPTUAL is allowed only if (a) generated from the approved style frame and model sheet, (b) every frame is retouched to the rules, and (c) the class is recorded in `discovery.ts`. Never photoreal; never REAL (no photographs of children).

## 18. ACCESSIBILITY ROLE

Alt: „Ein Kind sitzt auf einer langen Bank und schaut in ein Buch. Neben ihm sitzt eine erwachsene Person.“

## 19. WHAT NOT TO GENERATE

- A clock, numbers, a ticket or queue display, a call screen.
- Toys, a play corner, other children or patients (no implied practice waiting room).
- A bored, impatient or sad child; a phone or tablet in the child's hands.
- The companion's face.

## 20. CONSISTENCY

The bench uses the arch's ceramic; the cushion is the coral of the ENTDECKEN half-disc. The figures are identical to 01.
