## 1. PURPOSE

Explains looking into the ears with a small lamp (the copy also mentions the throat). Job: *a small light helps to see.*

## 2. STORY MOMENT

Close: the child sits upright, seen in ¾ profile from the child's right side; a small lamp (otoscope) held by a sleeved hand glows warm **beside** the ear, about to look. Copy: „Mit einer kleinen Lampe kann die Ärztin in deine Ohren schauen.“

## 3. EMOTION

Still and calm, eyes forward, relaxed mouth (closed).

## 4. PRIMARY SUBJECT

**Otoscope lamp**: ceramic handle with a brass ring and a small cone tip with a warm glow, at C8–C9 × R3–R4 (tip at x 1500, y 700). It is held 120 px **away** from the ear, never inside it. Handle length ~460 px, angled 20° up toward the ear.

## 5. SECONDARY OBJECTS

- **Child**: head and shoulders, head ≈ 520 px (x 700–1220, top y 200), ¾ profile facing left so the right ear (x ~1180, y 680) faces the lamp; hair tucked or short so the ear is visible; yellow top.
- **Hand and sleeve**: from the right edge (C10–C12 × R4–R5), ceramic sleeve as in 04.
- **Light cone** (fx): a soft warm cone from the tip toward the ear, fading out before the ear.

## 6. ENVIRONMENT

Night stage only. No room, no couch, no instrument tray.

## 7. COMPOSITION

Child's head on the left third; the lamp's glow is the brightest point between head and right edge. The gaze line runs from the eye forward-left (calm), the light line from the right to the ear.

## 8. CAMERA ANGLE

Child head height, 50 mm, ¾ profile, 0° tilt.

## 9. LIGHT

**Two sources:** the key from the upper right (shared) and the lamp tip (practical, warm, small radius). The ear receives a gentle warm rim from the lamp. Cobalt fill on the back of the head.

## 10. COLOR

## 11. MATERIAL

Ceramic and brass instrument; small matte-glass tip (the only glass in the story); felt clothes.

## 12. NEGATIVE SPACE

## 13. DESKTOP ASPECT / CROP

## 14. MOBILE ASPECT / CROP

## 15. SEPARATE LAYERS

## 16. POTENTIAL MOTION

Optional, once: the lamp glow switches on (0 → 100 % over 0.6 s), and the cone fades in after it (0.8 s). There is no flicker. The end frame equals the master.

## 17. CLASSIFICATION

**ORIGINAL ART preferred** (commissioned illustrator/3D artist working to the style frame). AI_CONCEPTUAL is allowed only if (a) generated from the approved style frame and model sheet, (b) every frame is retouched to the rules, and (c) the class is recorded in `discovery.ts`. Never photoreal; never REAL (no photographs of children).

## 18. ACCESSIBILITY ROLE

Alt: „Eine kleine Lampe leuchtet neben dem Ohr eines Kindes.“

## 19. WHAT NOT TO GENERATE

- The lamp inside the ear; an ear canal, eardrum or inner view.
- An open mouth, tongue depressor or throat view (the throat stays in the text only).
- A head being held, tilted by force, or restrained.
- The doctor's face or body; a head mirror; clinical light.

## 20. CONSISTENCY

The same sleeve and hand as in 04 (the only „doctor“ element in the story). The lamp glow uses the arch's warm light colour, so it reads as „friendly light“.
