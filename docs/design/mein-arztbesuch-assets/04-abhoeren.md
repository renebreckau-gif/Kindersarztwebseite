## 1. PURPOSE

Explains listening with a stethoscope and prepares the child for a cool chest piece. Job: *heart and breath make sounds; this instrument hears them.*

## 2. STORY MOMENT

Close: the cobalt chest piece of a stethoscope rests on the child's T-shirt at the chest. A hand in a warm ceramic-coloured sleeve holds it from the upper right. The child looks down at it. Copy: „Damit kann sie hören, wie dein Herz und deine Atmung klingen. Das Stethoskop kann sich etwas kühl anfühlen.“

## 3. EMOTION

Curious and calm, observing; breath held lightly, as if listening too.

## 4. PRIMARY SUBJECT

**Stethoscope chest piece**: round cobalt enamel disc (Ø 300 px) with a brass rim at x 1080, y 1080 (C5–C6 × R5–R6), resting on the T-shirt. The dark tubing curves up and out of frame at the top right (x ~1900, y 0). The ear tubes are **outside** the frame.

## 5. SECONDARY OBJECTS

- **Child**: head and upper body, head top y 160, head ≈ 420 px (x 760–1180), torso to the bottom edge; ¾ view facing right, eyes looking down at the chest piece; yellow T-shirt (no print).
- **Hand and sleeve**: enters from the upper right (C9–C12 × R3–R5), sleeve warm ceramic #EEE9DF (no white coat, no cuff detail), relaxed fingers on the chest-piece stem. There is no face and no body beyond the forearm.
- **Sound arcs** (fx): two very soft concentric arcs to the right of the chest piece (radius 220 / 360 px, 2 px lines at 30 % opacity).

## 6. ENVIRONMENT

Night stage only; no floor visible (close-up). No examination couch, no paper roll, no room.

## 7. COMPOSITION

## 16. POTENTIAL MOTION

## 17. CLASSIFICATION

**ORIGINAL ART preferred** (commissioned illustrator/3D artist working to the style frame). AI_CONCEPTUAL is allowed only if (a) generated from the approved style frame and model sheet, (b) every frame is retouched to the rules, and (c) the class is recorded in `discovery.ts`. Never photoreal; never REAL (no photographs of children).

## 18. ACCESSIBILITY ROLE

Alt: „Ein Stethoskop liegt auf dem T-Shirt eines Kindes. Das Kind schaut neugierig darauf.“

## 19. WHAT NOT TO GENERATE

- A bare chest, a lifted T-shirt or undressing.
- A doctor's face or body, a white coat or a name badge.
- A heart symbol, ECG line, organs or X-ray look.
- A worried or squeezed face; a child holding their breath dramatically.
- A toy stethoscope or wrong anatomy of the instrument (the physician checks it).

## 20. CONSISTENCY

The chest piece is **the** cobalt element of the scene (one cobalt per scene). The child's T-shirt and head match the model sheet; the close-up uses the same light direction as the wide scenes.
