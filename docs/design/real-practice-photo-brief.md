# Real Practice Photography — Shot Brief

Phase: 08 · Date: 2026-10-07 · Status: **brief for the photographer; nothing shot yet**
Register: [required-production-assets.md](required-production-assets.md) (B1, B2, C1, C2, C3) · Style: [photography-direction.md](photography-direction.md) · Policy: [../architecture/media-policy.md](../architecture/media-policy.md)

Class for every asset here: **REAL**. These are real photographs of the real practice and the real physicians. They are never generated, never composited with AI people, never retouched beyond colour, exposure and removing dust or cables. They are a **separate track** from Mein Arztbesuch (D1): real photos never appear inside the story scenes, and story illustrations never pretend to be the practice.

Order of the shoot day: **B1 → B2 → C1** (priority P1, needed for launch), then **C2 → C3** (P2). One photographer, one day, one light setup for B1/B2.

Prerequisites (blocking): written consent and image rights for each person (LB-09, F11/F33). The practice confirms which rooms may be shown. The practice is closed or no patients are present during C2/C3.

Shared look: warm natural daylight from one side (as in the hero: light from the right where possible), soft falloff, plaster/paper/wood tones, no flash look, no clinical blue, no stock poses. Colour target: neutral-warm white balance (≈ 5000 K); skin tones natural, not orange.

## B1 — Portrait Nadine Probst

| Field | Brief |
|---|---|
| **ZIEL** | Parents see who will treat their child: calm, competent, approachable. Used on Praxis › Ärztinnen and in Start chapter 04. |
| **MOTIV** | Nadine Probst, head and shoulders to mid-torso, three-quarter turn of the body, face toward the camera, relaxed closed-mouth or slight smile. The hands are not in frame, or rest naturally. |
| **KAMERA** | Full frame, 85 mm (or 70–105), f/2.8–4, camera at eye height, distance ~2 m. Shoot horizontal and vertical. |
| **FRAMING** | 4 : 5 master. The eyes sit on the upper third line (y ≈ 33 %). Space above the head 8–12 %. The body turned 30° to the right, face to the camera. |
| **LICHT** | Window light from **camera right** (matches the site's light direction), soft (diffused through a sheer or white bounce on the left side for fill, ratio ≈ 1 : 2.5). No on-camera flash. |
| **HINTERGRUND** | A practice wall in plaster or warm white, 1.5–2 m behind the person, softly out of focus. No posters, certificates, logos or anatomical charts. |
| **OUTFIT/REALISMUS** | What she really wears at work. A white coat is optional, not required. No costume props (no stethoscope worn for effect unless it is her normal daily practice). Minimal retouching: no skin smoothing beyond temporary blemishes. |
| **NEGATIVE SPACE** | The left 15 % stays quiet (wall) so the card layout can breathe; no element touches the edges. |
| **DESKTOP CROP** | 4 : 5 card, ~12 rem column (approximately 384 × 480 css px). Export 1200 × 1500. |
| **MOBILE CROP** | The same 4 : 5, ~9 rem column; the face must remain ≥ 90 px tall. Also deliver a 1 : 1 crop centred on the face for small lists. |
| **PERSONEN IM HINTERGRUND** | None. |
| **DATENSCHUTZ** | Signed consent for web use (purpose, duration, revocation). No patient files, screens or names visible anywhere in frame. |
| **WAS VERMIEDEN WERDEN SOLL** | Arms crossed; pointing; a stethoscope "pose"; a heavy smile on command; a clinical white background; AI retouching or face changes; background blur created by AI. |

## C1 — Entrance / exterior

| Field | Brief |
|---|---|
| **ZIEL** | Parents arriving for the first time recognise the door. Used on Kontakt & Anfahrt and Neu bei uns (step 3). |
| **MOTIV** | The practice entrance as a parent sees it when arriving on foot: the door, the house number and the practice sign readable; a bit of the street or path for orientation. |
| **KAMERA** | 35 mm, camera at standing adult eye height (~160 cm), straight on or a slight angle (≤ 15°), vertical lines kept vertical (shift or correct in post). |
| **FRAMING** | Deliver both **3 : 2** (wide, more street context) and **4 : 5** (door-centred). The door is centred horizontally in 4 : 5 and on the right third in 3 : 2. |
| **LICHT** | Daylight, overcast or morning/late afternoon; no harsh noon shadows across the door; the door itself evenly lit. |
| **HINTERGRUND** | The real building. Remove temporary clutter (bins, bikes) physically before shooting, not digitally. |
| **OUTFIT/REALISMUS** | Unchanged reality: no added plants, signs or decorations. Only colour and perspective correction. |
| **NEGATIVE SPACE** | Sky or wall above the door (top 15 %) is calm for an optional caption. |
| **DESKTOP CROP** | 3 : 2 next to the contact list (export 2400 × 1600). |
| **MOBILE CROP** | 4 : 5 (export 1200 × 1500); the house number is ≥ 14 css px tall at 360 px width. |
| **PERSONEN IM HINTERGRUND** | None. Wait for an empty street. If unavoidable, people are unrecognisable (back view, far, out of focus); no faces, no children. |
| **DATENSCHUTZ** | Car number plates are not readable (choose an angle or time without parked cars; if unavoidable, blur plates). Neighbouring residents' windows show no private interiors. |
| **WAS VERMIEDEN WERDEN SOLL** | Wide-angle distortion; a dramatic sky; night shots; HDR look; AI sky replacement; any change to the sign or number (F13/LB-03 confirm the address first). |

## C2 — Reception (P2)

| Field | Brief |
|---|---|
| **ZIEL** | Reduce the unknown before the first visit: "this is where you register". Used on Neu bei uns and Praxis. |
| **MOTIV** | The reception counter from the entrance side at a parent's viewpoint, with the area where the parent will stand. Optionally a staff member **only with consent**, seen working naturally, not posing. Shoot a version **without people** in any case. |
| **KAMERA** | 28–35 mm, camera at 140 cm (between adult and child height), level, verticals corrected. |
| **FRAMING** | 3 : 2 master; the counter on the lower-left third; the room's depth leads to the right. |
| **LICHT** | Existing room light plus daylight; switch off coloured or flickering lights; no flash. Warm-neutral white balance. |
| **HINTERGRUND** | The real reception. Remove paper notices with names, appointment lists and personal items before shooting. |
| **OUTFIT/REALISMUS** | Real state on a normal (tidy) day; no staging furniture. |
| **NEGATIVE SPACE** | The upper right (ceiling or wall) is calm. |
| **DESKTOP CROP** | 3 : 2 editorial width (export 2400 × 1600). |
| **MOBILE CROP** | 4 : 5 centred on the counter (export 1200 × 1500). |
| **PERSONEN IM HINTERGRUND** | No patients and no parents. Staff only with written consent; otherwise the people-free version. |
| **DATENSCHUTZ** | **No screens, files, labels, calendars or insurance cards visible** — turn monitors off or away. Check every frame at 100 % zoom before delivery. |
| **WAS VERMIEDEN WERDEN SOLL** | Busy moments; queues; visible patient data; a fish-eye look; blurred-out faces (better: no faces). |

## C3 — Waiting area (P2)

| Field | Brief |
|---|---|
| **ZIEL** | Show a calm place to wait. Used on Neu bei uns and Praxis. It is not used inside Mein Arztbesuch (scene 02 is an illustration on purpose). |
| **MOTIV** | The real waiting area empty: seats, light and what is really there (only what the practice confirms. Do **not** add toys or books for the shot). |
| **KAMERA** | 28–35 mm at seated adult eye height (~110 cm), level. |
| **FRAMING** | 3 : 2 master; the window side to the right if possible (light from the right). |
| **LICHT** | Daylight dominant; room lights on only if they match warm-neutral. |
| **HINTERGRUND** | Remove notices with personal data, lost-and-found items and anything branded by third parties where possible. |
| **OUTFIT/REALISMUS** | Real furniture and real state on a tidy day. No styling props that are not normally there (this would be a false practice claim). |
| **NEGATIVE SPACE** | The upper third (wall or ceiling) is calm. |
| **DESKTOP CROP** | 3 : 2 (export 2400 × 1600). |
| **MOBILE CROP** | 4 : 5 (export 1200 × 1500), centred on the seating. |
| **PERSONEN IM HINTERGRUND** | None, ever. No children and no parents, even unrecognisable. |
| **DATENSCHUTZ** | As C2; also check for name lists on doors or pinboards. |
| **WAS VERMIEDEN WERDEN SOLL** | Added toys; staged plants; wide-angle exaggeration of space; any person; AI clean-up that invents surfaces. |
